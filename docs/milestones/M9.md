# M9 — Real hardware: step records

Each step's row is updated in the commit after it (a commit cannot name its own hash).

Not scoped past 9a. Decided 2026-10-08: the real PC leaves M6, where it was 6g, and opens this milestone, renamed from "Q8B platform". M6 then closes with `libvx` v0 and its freeze. The PC and the Q8B are both T1 and both boot with ACPI. The PC's step builds the bring-up that does not need a serial port, `drv-xhci` and ACPI's events, and the Q8B uses all three. The Q8B's rows (04 §6: `devmgr`'s spawn-time grants, the SoC and board records, `clock` and `gpio`, the MMU-500, the platform service, ADRs 0019–0022) come when it is scoped, after M8 gives its Adreno a GPU stack.
