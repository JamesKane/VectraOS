Scoped 2026-10-08: [docs/21](../21-pixels.md) holds the decisions (client-owned buffers, Flatland's credits on 03 §4's records, `displayd` and `winsrv` split as Fuchsia's coordinator and compositor, write-combining physical VMOs, HID input records with the held set, rio's per-app attach, v1's VRR/tearing/HDR fields carried but not driven), the contradictions it settles, and the order.
