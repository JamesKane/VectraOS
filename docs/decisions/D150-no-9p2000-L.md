# D150. No 9P2000.L: Linux mounts an export as 9P2000, and pjdfstest's failures outside it are recorded, not answered (2026-10-30, decided by the owner; amends D78, D148)

D78 left 9gate a translator that would speak `.L` toward Linux where
9P2000 fell short of `v9fs`'s conformance tests, and D148 made pjdfstest
over a `v9fs` mount the test that would decide which `.L` messages. It
ran (docs/v9fs-conformance.md): after two faults of the export were
fixed, every failure asks for what 9P2000 or Node has no word for
(symlinks, special files and hard links, numeric owners, modes and
permissions, atime and nanosecond times) or follows from one.

## Decided

- **9gate speaks no `.L`.** It is Linux's dialect, POSIX carried in 9P;
  VectraOS doesn't take it on to pass POSIX's tests. Linux mounts an
  export as 9P2000 (`version=9p2000`), as 9front does, and gets what
  9P2000 says.
- **pjdfstest stays the conformance check**, judged as D148 has it: what
  9P2000 can say passes, and each failure is listed in
  docs/v9fs-conformance.md by its cause as outside 9P2000 or Node. A
  failure that 9P2000 and Node could answer is a fault of the export, to
  be fixed.
- **Node's owners and modes**, if Node gains them, still come as D78 has
  them (string names, 9P2000's bits), and reach Linux through 9P2000.
